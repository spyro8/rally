Support

We usually reply within a day or two.

Get help

Email spyrapp.team@gmail.com, or open SPYR and tap Me → Send feedback. Your app version is included automatically.

Quick answers

I forgot my password

On the login screen, type your email and tap Forgot password?. We'll email a 6-digit code; type it in SPYR with your new password. You can also tap Email me a code instead to sign in without a password.

I didn't get the email

Check your spam and Promotions folders, wait a minute and try send it again. Codes expire after an hour.

I had an account before email sign-in

Tap Older account? on the login screen. Enter an email and password, then your old name and passcode (or recovery code). Forgot the passcode too? Email us your SPYR name and the email you used and we'll link it for you.

I started as a guest

Your account lives on that phone until you save it. Go to Me → Save account and add an email and password so you never lose it.

Inviting friends

Tap Invite on Home (or Send invite link after creating a team). Friends tap the link and their join code is filled in.

Delete my account

Me → Delete account. This permanently removes everything. See the privacy policy.
