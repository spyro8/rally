Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

SPYR is a habit app you use on your own or with a small team. This page explains, in plain words, what SPYR keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: SPYR keeps what you log so the app can work. No ads, no tracking across other apps or sites, and nothing is sold.

What SPYR keeps

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider). If you start as a guest, there's no email until you save your account.
  • Your profile: the name you choose, your avatar, character and crest choices, your team(s) and when you joined them.
  • What you log: steps, sleep, water, workouts, meals and calories, meditation, focus and fasting times, reading, and whether you journaled.
  • Optional health profile: weight, height, age, sex and activity level, only if you fill them in. They're used to show BMI, protein and calorie estimates.
  • What you write: Notebook entries and moods, MIND thoughts and tasks, books on your Shelf, team chat messages, and anything you send with "Send feedback".
  • Your rooms: your village, journey progress and gym history.
  • Usage counts: simple daily counts of how often you open the app, log a habit, or open each room. They help us see whether SPYR is useful. They're tied to your account and not shared with anyone.

Who can see it

  • Your teammates see your name, avatar, team and the points and habit totals that make up the team score, plus anything you post in team chat.
  • Other players may see your name and team on public team lists and leaderboards.
  • Only you can see your email, health profile, Notebook, MIND, Shelf notes and village.
  • Us: the SPYR team can access the database to keep the app running, fix problems and read feedback. We don't read your private entries unless you ask us to help with something specific.

Where it's stored

SPYR's data is stored with Supabase, a database and sign-in provider, on servers in the United States. Sign-in emails (codes and password resets) are sent through Google's email service. Some things, like music and display settings, are only kept on your phone.

Apple Health and other connections

If a future version of SPYR asks to read data from Apple Health or Health Connect, it will only read what you allow, only to fill in your daily logs. That data is never used for advertising and never sold or shared with data brokers.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting your data.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Me → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, logs, rooms, usage counts, your messages in team chat, and your sign-in. It can't be undone.
  • Change or correct things: edit your name, logs and health profile at any time in the app.
  • Questions or a copy of your data: email spyrapp.team@gmail.com and we'll help.

Kids

SPYR isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep data from them. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Not medical advice

Calorie, protein and BMI numbers in SPYR are general estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before big changes to diet or exercise.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and mention anything important in the app.
